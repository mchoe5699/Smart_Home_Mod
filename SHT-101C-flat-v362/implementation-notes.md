The recoloring/logo edit uses image generation, preserving the supplied flat elevation as its reference. Manufacturer marks are reconstructed artwork rather than a claim of an exact original vector logo. Release validation includes integrated-game front/oblique/profile captures and normal build, six-face constant-thickness geometry checks, archive resource checks, and downloaded publication byte comparison.
